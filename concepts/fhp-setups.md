# FHP Setups

## Dollar Breakout Scan (Dan Zanger)

- Stock up **$0.90+ from open** with minimum **100K volume** daily
- Dan Zanger: originally $1 from opening print
- This is the core scan — simple, daily

## Why Dollar Breakout?

- High price stocks ($100-$300+) break out with **1-2% moves** instead of 4%
- 4% breakout scans will miss many excellent FHP setups
- $1 move on $300 stock = 0.33% — still valid if sustained

## Position Sizing Advantage

- **2% or less stop** = dramatically larger position sizes
- $300 stock with $2 stop allows risking 80% of account on single trade
- Same dollar risk, much larger position = compound growth faster

## Entry Rules

- Stock must **close near high** of day (same as SOS rules)
- Wait for **momentum burst bar** confirming breakout
- SOS confirmation still required — this is a QUALIFIED breakout

## Stop Placement

- Stop at **low of the day** on high price stocks
- Risk is already inherently low on FHP
- 2.5% or less stop distance preferred

## FHP vs Sugar Babies

| | FHP | Sugar Babies |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $100-$300+ | $3-$15 |
| Stop | 2.5% | 10% |
| Move Duration | 5-10 days | Faster |
| Babysitting | Low | High |
| Risk/Reward | 5-10x | Variable |

## Sector Examples

- **Booking Holdings** (BKNG) — multiple setups per year
- **AutoZone** (AZO) — 0.95%-1.77% avg breakout
- **O'Reilly Automotive** (ORLY) — 1%-1.5% consistent
- **Home Depot** (HD) — 0.8% breakout, $3 risk → $32 potential

## Qualification Checklist

- [ ] Dollar breakout: up $0.90+ from open
- [ ] Volume: 100K+ shares
- [ ] High price stock: $100+
- [ ] Low percentage breakout (1-2%)
- [ ] Close near high of day
- [ ] SOS confirmation
- [ ] Stop can be placed at 2.5% or less
- [ ] Institutional participation likely

## Short Setups (FHP Breakdown)

- Same rules apply in reverse
- High price stocks when they break down have **very low risk**
- Asymmetric reward for shorts on breakdowns
