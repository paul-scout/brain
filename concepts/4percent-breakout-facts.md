# 4% Breakout Facts

## Data Foundation

- Study analyzed **all stocks making 25%+ moves within a month** over 40 years of data
- 40-year deep dive into stock behavior patterns
- Most 20%+ moves in 5 days **start with a 4% breakout on day one**

## Market Cap Distribution

- Only **193 stocks** with 20%+ moves in 5 days had market caps **above $10 billion**
- **1,100 stocks** making these moves had market caps **below $1 billion**
- Large caps are statistically unlikely to make 20%+ moves in 5 days

## Sector Distribution

- **Healthcare, tech, and consumer discretionary** dominate the 20%+ in 5 days category
- These sectors have the highest concentration of explosive movers

## Real Trade Examples

### INBS (Indiana)

- Moved **99% in 3-4 days** starting with a 4% breakout
- Moved **83% in a couple of days** after another 4% breakout
- Moved **148%** following a 4% breakout pattern
- Demonstrates the compounding effect of multiple 4% breakouts

## Key Statistics

- Most 20%+ moves in 5 days start with a 4% breakout on day one
- 4% breakouts attract additional buying for approximately **3-5 sessions**
- Below $1 billion market cap = highest probability of big moves
- Healthcare, tech, consumer discretionary = dominant sectors

## The Math

- 20% move in 5 days = 4% per day average
- This means at least one 4% breakout day is mathematically required
- Impossible for a stock to make 20%+ move in 5 days without showing a 4% day

## Conversion to Tradeable Insight

- If a stock can make a 20%+ move in 5 days, it MUST have a 4% day
- Therefore, finding all 4% breakouts captures virtually all explosive movers
- The qualification criteria filter which 4% breakouts are worth trading
