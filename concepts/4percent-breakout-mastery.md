# 4% Breakout Mastery

## The Power of Simplicity

- One scan, one setup, executed with **conviction over years** can build a trading career
- Complexity is NOT required for success
- Stockbee traded ONLY the 4% breakout for the first 8-10 years of his career
- Simplifying to just one scan can be sufficient for a **trading career**

## The Mathematical Inevitability

- A 20% move in 5 days mathematically requires at least one 4% day (20/5=4)
- It is **impossible** for big moves to occur without this signal appearing
- This makes the 4% breakout a reliable discovery mechanism

## Qualification Is the Edge

- The 4% breakout alone is NOT the edge
- The **qualification criteria** (SOS, low volatility, sector, market cap) transform a signal into a tradeable setup
- Without proper qualification, most 4% breakouts fail to follow through

## Probability Stacking

- Each qualification criterion incrementally increases the probability of success:
  - Low volatility +10%
  - Sector alignment +10%
  - Market cap below $1B +15%
  - Linear price action +10%
  - SOS confirmation +20%
- The edge comes from **stacking probabilities**

## Signal vs. Setup

- **Signal**: Something is happening in the market
- **Setup**: High-probability tradeable opportunity
- The 4% breakout is merely a signal
- Qualification turns it into a setup

## Belief as a Career Definer

- Stockbee: "Without finding this one simple concept, I would have remained in corporate life rather than becoming a trader"
- **Belief in the concept** is more important than just knowing about it
- conviction + consistency > complexity

## What Makes It Work

- 4% breakout attracts additional buyers for 3-5 sessions
- The initial move creates **momentum and attention**
- Subsequent buyers chase, pushing price further
- Low volatility before = accumulation, not distribution

## Why Most Traders Miss It

- They overcomplicate with too many indicators
- They don't have conviction in a single concept
- They apply it inconsistently due to lack of belief
- They trade it as a signal without qualification

## Focus Principle

- One setup mastered deeply > multiple setups executed poorly
- Master the 4% breakout + its qualifications, execute with conviction
