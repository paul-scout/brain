# 4% Breakout Setups

## The Signal vs. Setup Distinction

- **4% breakout** = merely a signal that something is happening
- **Tradeable setup** = signal + qualification criteria
- The edge is NOT in the 4% rule alone — it is in the QUALIFICATION

## The 4% Rule

- A **4% breakout** (up or down) is the triggering event
- Works in both directions: breakout for upside, breakdown for downside
- Same 4% signal applies regardless of stock price ($10 or $300 stock)
- Attracts additional buying for approximately **3-5 sessions** after the signal

## Start of Swing (SOS) — Critical Qualification

- The 4% move must be at the **beginning of a new move** (SOS)
- Not a continuation of an existing move
- This is the single most important qualification criterion

## Low Volatility Before Breakout

- Low volatility periods **prior to breakout** produce the most tradeable setups
- Contraction before expansion = highest probability
- High volatility before breakout = distribution risk

## Sector Qualification

- **Healthcare, tech, and consumer discretionary** dominate 20%+ movers
- Focusing on these sectors increases probability

## Market Cap Qualification

- **Below $10 billion** = increased probability of making money
- **Below $1 billion** = highest probability of big moves
- Large caps (>10B) rarely make 20%+ moves in 5 days

## Price/Linearity Qualification

- Linear/orderly price action = easier trade management
- Choppy stocks can still move, but harder to manage
- Linearity affects trade management even if not the primary filter

## Qualification Checklist

- [ ] 4% breakout (up or down)
- [ ] Start of Swing (new move, not continuation)
- [ ] Low volatility consolidation before breakout
- [ ] Sector: healthcare, tech, or consumer discretionary
- [ ] Market cap below $10B (preferably below $1B)
- [ ] Linear/orderly price action (preferred)

## Why 4%?

- 40 years of stock data deep dive by Stockbee
- Mathematical inevitability: a 20% move in 5 days mathematically requires at least one 4% day (20/5=4)
- Impossible for big moves to occur without this signal appearing

## Scan

- Simple daily scan: **4% breakout + volume condition**
- That is sufficient for a trading career
