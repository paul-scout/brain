# Cody Schneider — Marketing Channels AI Agents Can Run

## SUMMARY

Cody Schneider provides a comprehensive taxonomy of marketing channels that AI agents can already automate for businesses, covering paid ads, organic social, content/SEO, email, community, and partnerships.

## ONE-SENTENCE TAKEAWAY

Diverse, integrated marketing strategies leveraging AI are essential for maximizing audience reach and business engagement.

---

## IDEAS

- Meta ads offer the largest reach for B2C and DTC, making them indispensable in digital marketing.
- Google ads capture high-intent audiences, proving effective at targeting consumers in the purchasing phase.
- YouTube ads provide a hybrid model, combining awareness with direct response for marketing strategies.
- TikTok advertising currently benefits from lower CPMs and engages a younger demographic effectively.
- LinkedIn ads, though expensive, offer the best targeting capabilities for B2B marketing strategies.
- LinkedIn delivers the highest organic reach for businesses focused on B2B marketing.
- Twitter/X serves as an effective platform for individuals aiming to build thought leadership and engage tech audiences.
- TikTok and Instagram Reels excel in video-driven, algorithmic engagement, maximizing reach and visibility.
- YouTube remains vital for long-tail SEO, enabling businesses to benefit from compounding views.
- Blog/SEO remains essential for driving compounding, high-intent traffic to a business website.
- AEO (answer engine optimization) reflects a significant shift in SEO dynamics influenced by artificial intelligence.
- YouTube maintains its position as the second largest search engine, important for video content marketing.
- Newsletters offer the highest ROI among digital marketing channels, benefiting business audience retention.
- Cold email functions as the backbone of B2B outbound marketing efforts, establishing initial connections.
- Lifecycle and nurture email sequences support customer retention and upsell efforts effectively.
- Referral programs capitalize on community and word-of-mouth marketing for organic growth.
- Slack and Discord communities can nurture dedicated audiences, encouraging deeper customer/business relationships.
- Reddit and niche forums facilitate direct community engagement, fostering authentic discussions and brand loyalty.
- Affiliate and influencer partnerships can expand brand reach and enhance customer trust and reliability.
- Co-marketing initiatives assist businesses in expanding their audience base by pooling resources.
- Integration partnerships, particularly B2B, offer strategic value in strengthening business alliances.
- Podcast sponsorships provide targeted exposure to niche audiences, enhancing marketing campaign versatility.
- The emergence of AI-driven search represents a transformative change, challenging traditional SEO practices.
- AI solutions redefine digital marketing, reinforcing a shift toward more tailored, intelligent consumer engagement models.

---

## INSIGHTS

- The dominance of AI-driven search is revolutionizing traditional SEO, necessitating adaptive marketing strategies for businesses.
- Email marketing retains high ROI, making it indispensable for maintaining customer engagement and promoting loyal relationships.
- Developing co-marketing strategies allows brands to extend their reach and resource-sharing capabilities.
- The power of LinkedIn in B2B marketing remains unmatched, providing remarkable organic reach and engagement.
- Effective use of TikTok's low CPMs strategically taps into younger demographics, driving brand relevance.
- YouTube's growing importance within SEO strategies signals a shift toward more video-centric content.
- The strategic importance of referral programs lies in capitalizing on community-driven, word-of-mouth marketing.
- Traditional SEO's role diminishes with the surge of AI, urging businesses to prioritize answer engine optimization efforts.
- YouTube ads serve as a valuable hybrid approach, marrying awareness with conversion goals in marketing strategies.
