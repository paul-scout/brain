# FHP Facts

## Dollar Breakout Scan Criteria

- Stock up **$0.90 from open**
- Minimum **100,000 shares volume** daily
- Originally Dan Zanger used **$1 from opening print**

## High Price Stock Breakout Percentages

- High price stocks typically break out with **1-2% moves** (vs 4% for lower priced)
- AutoZone: **0.95% to 1.77%** average breakout range
- O'Reilly Automotive: **1% to 1.5%** consistent
- Home Depot: **0.8%** breakout with $3 risk

## Position Sizing Mathematics

| Stock Price | Stop % | Stop $ | Position Size (per $10K acct) |
|---|---|---|---|
| $300 | 2% | $6 | $1,667 (16.7%) |
| $300 | 0.67% | $2 | $5,000 (50%) |
| $30 | 10% | $3 | $3,333 (33%) |
| $5 | 20% | $1 | $10,000 (100%) ← danger |

## Why 2% Stop Changes Everything

- 10% stop limits you to 10% of account
- 2% stop allows 50%+ of account
- $300 stock with $2 stop = can deploy **significantly larger % of capital**
- Same risk dollar, 5x+ more capital deployed
