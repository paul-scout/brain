# SOS Continuation Setups

## First Leg Qualification

- First leg must show **15%+ movement** with persistent buying
- **Linearity** in price action = strong buying interest at every level
- Initial momentum leg reveals institutional conviction

## Consolidation Phase

- Duration: **3-10 days** (NOT just 1 day)
- Single day pause = insufficient commitment
- **Low volatility** during consolidation = orderly profit-taking (NOT panic selling)
- High/volatile consolidation = distribution, not accumulation

## Continuation Direction

- Works in **both long AND short** directions
- Momentum trading in either direction is valid

## Entry Rules

- Buy **first or second leg** moves → better risk-reward
- **Third/fourth leg** = tighter stops, lower profit expectations, exhaustion risk
- Stock must **close near high** at 9:33-9:35 entry time
- Never buy fading price action

## Weak Structure Short Setup

- Requires **3 consecutive down days** (waterfall decline)
- Each bounce day must be **< 2.5%** to qualify
- Look for **weak bounce attempts** after forced selling capitulation

## Price Qualification

- Stocks over $60: minimum **$10 move**
- Stocks at $300: **$20-30 move** qualification
- Avoid stocks below $3

## Volume Requirements

- Minimum **9 million volume** for continuation longs
- Note: ADR and dollar volume filters eliminate neglected stocks BEFORE catalyst events

## Context Rules

- **Continuation longs**: avoid when market breadth is negative
- **Weak structure shorts**: work regardless of market breadth
- Short positions: enter in **last 15 minutes** only, close stops to minimize overnight risk

## Visual Pattern Recognition

- Study **300 examples** of each setup type
- Print them out for multiple reviews
- Embed patterns into **subconscious memory**

## Resume Signal

- **Range expansion** or **momentum burst bar** signals trend resumption
- 9:33-9:35 timing for entries confirmed
